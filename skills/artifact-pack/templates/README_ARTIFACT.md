## 5. Measurement notes

- Ratios are computed from interleaved A/B runs in one session (median of n).
- Absolute times depend on the machine; compare ratios and exact counts.
- Every run verifies its proof and checks the witness against a reference
  computation, and aborts on mismatch.
- Prover times <include / exclude> witness generation; setup and key generation are
  timed separately (`expected/preprocessing.csv`).
